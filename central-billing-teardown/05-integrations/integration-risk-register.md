# Integration risk register

Snapshot `a95b491e`.

| ID | Integration | Risk | Evidence | Likelihood | Impact | Severity | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IR-01 | Razorpay | **No request timeout.** The `razorpay` SDK calls `requests` without one, so a hung Razorpay connection holds a web worker or a billing worker until the job timeout (3,000 s on the `billing` queue). During a run, each hung charge removes one of the workers that set the charge rate. | `gateways/razorpay_adapter.py:65` (client built with auth only; no `timeout` anywhere in the file) | Low per call; real during a Razorpay incident | Billing run stalls; customer "Pay now" spins for minutes | P1 | Wrap the client session with a timeout (for example `client.session.request = partial(..., timeout=30)`) and map timeouts to `GatewayTimeout` |
| IR-02 | ERPNext | **Sync gives up after 3 tries and nothing retries it again.** Retries are driven by the hourly job, so the 3 attempts cover about 2–3 hours. An ERPNext outage longer than that leaves every invoice paid in the window at `erpnext_sync_status = Failed`. An alert fires; there is no automatic retry and no Desk button. | `ingester/erpnext_sync.py:22-23,100-131`; `platform/alerts.py:287`; no JS on Invoice for sync | Medium (any multi-hour ERPNext downtime) | Tax invoices missing for paid customers; manual console work per invoice | P1 | Keep retrying Failed with a long backoff (hours, then daily) and add a "Retry accounting sync" button and a bulk action |
| IR-03 | All gateways | Webhook events that fail 5 times stay Failed with no reprocess action. | `payments/webhooks.py:20,217-237`; no `webhook_event.js` | Low | A captured payment never settles its invoice until an engineer replays it; customer may be dunned | P2 | Add a Desk action to reprocess one event (issue 131) |
| IR-04 | Stripe | `stripe.max_network_retries = 2` is set on the global module, not on a client. Any other code in the process that uses `stripe` inherits it. | `gateways/stripe_adapter.py:180-181` | Low | Surprising retries elsewhere | P3 | Use a per-request `StripeClient` (issue 138) |
| IR-05 | ERPNext | Tax computed twice; payment entry posts `min(amount, outstanding)` | see R-01 in [calculation-risks.md](../04-financial-integrity/calculation-risks.md) | Medium | Paise drift, silent | P2 | Send Central's tax rows; compare totals |
| IR-06 | Atlas | Pause, suspension and restore depend on Atlas stopping or starting the server. Pause rolls back if the stop fails; restore after payment queues a start and logs a failure title if it cannot. | `catalog/subscriptions.py:1134-1146,1214-1240` | Medium | Paid customer whose server does not come back; support ticket | P2 | Show "starting your servers" state to the customer and alert ops when a restore start fails |
| IR-07 | Gateways ↔ console | Manual Checkout "Pay now" always opens the Razorpay sheet, but the backend opens the order on the invoice currency's default gateway. | `dashboard/src/composables/usePayInvoiceCheckout.ts:41`; `payments/charges.py:463-467` | Low today (INR default is Razorpay) | Pay now breaks for any non-Razorpay currency in Manual Checkout | P2 | Branch on `adapter_key` as the top-up flow already does (`useTopup.ts`) |
| IR-08 | Pilot facade | 25 guest endpoints depend on one decorator. Each currently has it. | `api/billing_api.py` (all 25 checked) | Low | A new endpoint without it would be open | P3 | Keep the Guard rule; add a test that every guest endpoint in the module carries the decorator |
| IR-09 | ERPNext | Invoices are held until the customer is synced and the GSTIN checked. A stuck customer sync holds the invoice and its collection. | `Invoice.hold_reason`; `ingester/customer.py` | Medium | Late collection; the customer sees "being prepared" | P2 | Alert on holds older than N hours (partly exists: held invoices are in operator alerts) |
| IR-10 | Grove, Cargo | Usage reported after the invoice for that period is drafted has no defined path except rerating. | `revenue/metering.py:287` (totals only rise); `api/admin/rerating.py` | Unknown | Unbilled or late-billed usage | P2 | Decide and document: carry late usage to the next invoice, or block reports for closed periods |
